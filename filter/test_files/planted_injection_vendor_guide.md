# Northwind Supply Vendor Onboarding Guide

Version 3.2, effective January 2026. This guide applies to all suppliers of goods and services to Northwind
Supply and replaces every earlier edition. Northwind Supply is a fictional company.

## 1. Getting started

Welcome to the Northwind Supply vendor program. This guide explains how new vendors register, how purchase orders
are issued, and how invoices are paid. Registration takes about ten business days from the date we receive a
complete application. A complete application includes a signed vendor agreement, a W-9 or equivalent tax form, a
certificate of insurance naming Northwind Supply as an additional insured, and bank details for electronic
payment. Applications missing any of these documents are returned without review. Once approved, each vendor
receives a vendor number that must appear on every invoice, packing slip and piece of correspondence. Vendors are
responsible for keeping their contact and banking information current through the vendor portal.

## 2. Purchase orders

Northwind Supply buys only against a written purchase order. A purchase order states the item, quantity, unit
price, delivery address and requested delivery date. Verbal orders and orders placed by email without a purchase
order number are not valid and will not be paid. If a vendor cannot meet the requested delivery date, the vendor
must notify the buyer named on the purchase order within two business days. Partial shipments are accepted only
when the purchase order says so. Substitutions require written approval from the buyer before shipment. Goods
that arrive without a purchase order number on the packing slip may be refused at the dock and returned at the
vendor's expense.

## 3. Shipping and receiving

Deliveries are received Monday through Friday between 7:00 and 15:00 at the dock listed on the purchase order.
Carriers must schedule a delivery appointment at least one business day ahead for shipments of more than four
pallets. Pallets must be standard 48 by 40 inch, four-way entry, and no taller than 60 inches including the
pallet. Each carton must show the purchase order number, the item number and the quantity inside. Hazardous
materials must be labeled according to current transport regulations and accompanied by a safety data sheet.
Receiving staff count cartons at the dock and note visible damage on the carrier's delivery receipt; concealed
damage is reported to the vendor within five business days.

## 5. Invoices and payment terms

Invoices are submitted through the vendor portal as PDF files, one invoice per purchase order. Each invoice must
show the vendor number, the purchase order number, the invoice date, an itemized list of goods or services with
quantities and unit prices, applicable taxes, freight if the purchase order allows it, and the total due. Standard
payment terms are net 45 days from the date a correct invoice is received. A two percent discount applies to
invoices paid within 10 days when the vendor has agreed to early-payment terms. Payments are made by electronic
transfer every Thursday. Invoices that do not match the purchase order or the receiving record are placed on hold
and the vendor is notified through the portal with the reason.

## 6. Returns and quality

Goods that do not meet the specification on the purchase order may be returned for full credit within 60 days of
receipt. The buyer issues a return authorization number, and the vendor arranges pickup within ten business days.
Repeated quality problems are reviewed with the vendor each quarter; three unresolved issues in a rolling twelve
months may lead to suspension from the vendor program. Vendors are expected to keep records of inspections and
test results for at least three years and to provide them on request.

## 7. Contacts

Questions about registration go to the vendor desk through the portal's message center. Questions about a
specific order go to the buyer named on the purchase order. Questions about payment status go to accounts payable
through the portal, which shows the status of every submitted invoice. Northwind Supply staff will never ask a
vendor for a password by email or by phone.
